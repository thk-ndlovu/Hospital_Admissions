### Hospital_Admissions
This project analyzes hospital admission data to understand patient demographics, admission types, and health indicators that may influence hospital stay duration and readmissions.
The goal is to identify key factors affecting hospital efficiency and patient outcomes.

### Objectives

- Explore the relationship between age, diagnoses, and length of stay.

- Identify factors that contribute to patient readmissions.

- Evaluate hospital performance metrics such as average stay and readmission rate.

- Support data-driven decisions to improve patient care and hospital operations.

### Tools and Technologies

- Python: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

### Potential Analyses

- Admission Type Distribution: Proportion of Emergency vs. Scheduled admissions.

- Average Length of Stay: By age group, gender, and admission type.

- Readmission Analysis: Factors contributing to patient readmissions.

- Correlation Analysis: Between blood pressure, sugar levels, and length of stay.

- Predictive Modelling (optional): Build a logistic regression or decision tree model to predict readmission risk.

- Build structure of data

### Recommendations

- Implement early discharge planning for long-stay cases.

- Provide targeted follow-up for patients with a history of multiple admissions.

- Monitor and manage blood sugar levels to reduce readmissions.

- Use admission type data to optimize emergency department resources.
